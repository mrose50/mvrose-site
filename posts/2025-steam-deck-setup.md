As an enthusiastic supporter of the Steam Deck, I have dedicated considerable time to exploring the best hardware modifications, software enhancements, and accessories to optimize my overall experience. I would like to share my current Steam Deck OLED setup in hopes of inspiring others to enhance their own experience.

## Hardware Modifications

**2TB M.2 2230 NVMe SSD** - Upgrading from my standard 1 TB drive to this new one was a straightforward process. I successfully cloned my drive using an NVMe SSD Enclosure, following the detailed instructions provided in this Reddit thread.

**GuliKit Hall Effect Joysticks** - The new joysticks feel incredibly smooth, alleviating any concerns about stick drift. I found the upgrade to be surprisingly straightforward, especially after following this iFixit video tutorial.

**JSAUX Transparent RGB Back Plate** - This upgrade focused on aesthetics rather than just functionality, but it appears to help keep my Steam Deck running cooler.

**1 TB Micro SD Card** - This card holds all of my ROMs for emulation.

**Deckbuttons** - Replacing your Steam Deck buttons can be somewhat challenging, depending on your level of experience. However, the end result is well worth the effort, as they look fantastic once completed.

## Software Enhancements

**Decky Loader** - homebrew plugin launcher for the Steam Deck. Some of my favorite plugins are:

- **Audio Loader** - allows you to replace Steam UI sounds and add music when outside of a game.
- **AutoFlatpaks** - allows you to manage flatpaks on your Steam Deck without having to navigate to Desktop Mode.
- **HLTB for Deck** - displays game lengths according to How Long To Beat.
- **ProtonDB Badges** - displays ProtonDB badges based on how games will run on the Steam Deck.
- **SteamGridDB** - allows you to change Steam artwork while in Gaming Mode.
- **VibrantDeck** - allows you to adjust screen saturation and vibrancy.

**EmuDeck** - a tool that allows me to emulate older games on my Steam Deck.

**Non-Steam Apps** - in Desktop Mode, you have the ability to add Non-Steam applications to your library, allowing you to launch them seamlessly in Gaming Mode. Here are some of my personal favorites:

- **Firefox Browser** - allows you to access a web browser in Gaming Mode. I use this primarily to watch Netflix.
- **Discord** - allows you to access your Discord messages in Gaming Mode.
- **GeForce Now** - enables you to play games with enhanced settings that are not supported by the native hardware of the Steam Deck.

## Accessories

**NuPhy Air75 V2** - wireless mechanical keyboard, elegantly designed with a sleek carrying case that doubles as a stand for your Steam Deck.

**Logitech MX Master 3s** - wireless mouse that supports multiple profiles to be used across your devices.

**8Bitdo Ultimate 2.4G Wireless Controller** - wireless controller with a dedicated charging stand.

**Steam Deck Docking Station** - docking station that I use to connect my Steam Deck to my TV.

**Mechanism Gaming Pillow** - pillow designed for the Steam Deck that allows you to play comfortably lying down on the couch.

**Mechanism Phone Mount** - phone mount that allows you to watch videos on your phone while playing games on your Steam Deck.

**PlayVital Purple Back Buttons** - soft and textured back buttons that add additional comfort while playing.

**JSAUX Screen Protector** - protection from your screen getting scratched.

**Skull & Co. Thumb Grips** - Thumb grips that add additional comfort to your thumb sticks.

**JSAUX Silicone Grip Protection Case** - protective case that is designed to highlight the RGB back plate.

**Project Killswitch** - Premium protection that improves the Steam Deck experience.

## The Results

There are a wide array of options for customizing your Steam Deck. While these enhancements are not essential, they significantly enhance your overall gaming experience.
